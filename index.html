FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. No private consultant can guarantee a government visa decision. Final approval or refusal is made by the relevant authority.

Can I enquire for any country?

Yes. Use “Other Country” in the enquiry form if your destination is not shown in the popular list.

Do I need to pay before submitting an enquiry?

No. This website’s initial enquiry form is designed to be free. Any future paid service should be explained clearly before payment.

Where should official visa applications be submitted?

Applications should be submitted only through the relevant government, embassy, consulate, authorized visa centre or other official channel for that country.
